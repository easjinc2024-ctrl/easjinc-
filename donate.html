Donation policy

EASJ INC accepts donations in cash, in-kind goods, and strategic support from individuals, groups, and organizations that share our mission of justice, fairness, accountability, and community empowerment.

  • All donations are acknowledged and recorded.
  • In-kind donations are documented with description, quantity, and estimated value.
  • Support can be directed toward general operations, education, advocacy, or community support programs.
  • We exercise care and transparency in handling all donations and support materials.
We are committed to accountable, respectful, and transparent stewardship of every contribution.
