Accountability, equal opportunity, institutional integrity, and social justice for all.

Who We Are

Education Accountability and Social Justice (EASJ INC) is a Liberian nonprofit advocacy organization established on January 8, 2024. Its organizational mission is to promote accountability, advance justice, empower communities, and strengthen institutions.


Through public education, research, civic participation, community engagement, and responsible advocacy, EASJ INC seeks to contribute to a fairer, more transparent, inclusive, and accountable society.


The organization promotes peaceful civic action, respect for human rights, ethical leadership, and meaningful participation in decisions affecting communities.

Our Mission

To advance education accountability and social justice by promoting transparency, defending human rights, empowering citizens, and encouraging responsible leadership and effective public institutions.


Our Vision

A Liberia where justice, fairness, freedom, quality education, human dignity, and accountable governance are respected and accessible to everyone.


Our Tagline

Our Organizational Commitment

EASJ INC seeks to serve the public interest through constructive engagement, responsible reporting, community education, and evidence-informed advocacy.

Our 12 Advocacy Pillars

Twelve interconnected priorities guide our commitment to responsible governance, social justice, and community empowerment.

01

Education Accountability

Promoting quality education, transparent school governance, responsible education financing, teacher accountability, and equal learning opportunities.

02

Transparency and Anti-Corruption

Advocating integrity, openness, responsible public spending, access to public information, and measures to prevent corruption, bribery, and abuse of public office.

03

Human Rights and Dignity

Defending fundamental rights, human dignity, equality before the law, freedom, and respect for every person.

04

Rule of Law and Justice

Supporting fair legal processes, equal protection, access to justice, due process, and accountable institutions.

05

Gender Equality and SGBV Prevention

Promoting gender equality and advocating prevention of sexual and gender-based violence, exploitation, discrimination, and abuse.

06

Child Protection and Welfare

Advocating children's safety, protection from abuse, access to education, child welfare, and respect for children's rights.

07

Youth Empowerment and Leadership

Encouraging youth leadership, civic responsibility, skills development, meaningful participation, and opportunities for young people.

08

Disability Inclusion and Social Protection

Promoting accessibility, equal participation, inclusion of persons with disabilities, social protection, and dignity for vulnerable groups.

09

Democracy and Civic Education

Advancing civic and voter education, informed citizenship, peaceful democratic participation, and public accountability.

10

Environmental Justice

Promoting environmental responsibility, community protection, sustainable development, and accountability for environmental harm.

11

Public Health and Community Welfare

Supporting public awareness, community well-being, access to essential services, and the protection of vulnerable people.

12

Anti-Bullying, Peace and Social Cohesion

Promoting respect, peaceful coexistence, anti-bullying awareness, conflict prevention, and community solidarity.

Advocate Amen Saakollie Sheriff

Founder, CEO & Executive Director


Advocate Amen Saakollie Sheriff founded EASJ INC to advance education accountability, social justice, civic awareness, and responsible governance in Liberia.


The Office of the Founder and Executive Director provides organizational leadership and promotes the organization's mission, advocacy priorities, public engagement, partnerships, and institutional development.


Through constructive public engagement and responsible advocacy, the organization seeks to strengthen civic participation and encourage institutions to act with integrity and accountability.
