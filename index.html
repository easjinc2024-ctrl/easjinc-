Who We Are

Education Accountability and Social Justice (EASJ INC) is a civic organization committed to advancing justice, empowering communities, and strengthening institutions in Liberia and beyond.

We work to ensure that education, governance, and human rights systems are accountable, inclusive, and responsive to the people they serve.
