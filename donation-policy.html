Donation Policy

EASJ INC is a non-governmental, non-political organization committed to advancing justice, accountability, education, and community empowerment. We welcome support from individuals, organizations, and partners who share our mission and values.

1. Types of support

We accept the following forms of support:

  • Cash donations
  • In-kind donations
  • Program sponsorships
  • Volunteer support and technical contributions
  • Strategic partnerships

2. Donation acknowledgment

All donations are acknowledged and recorded. Donors will receive a confirmation of receipt when support is received or a pledge is accepted. For in-kind contributions, a record is created documenting the item(s), quantity, and estimated value.

3. Stewardship and transparency

EASJ INC is committed to responsible stewardship of funds and goods. Donations are used in line with the organization’s mission, programs, and approved priorities. We do not use funds for unrelated or non-mission purposes.

4. Use of in-kind donations

In-kind donations are reviewed based on relevance, need, value, and program suitability. Every item received is logged and assigned to a program or operational purpose when appropriate.

5. Donor information

Donor information is used solely for acknowledging support, communication, partnership follow-up, and internal record keeping. We respect donor privacy and take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us.

6. Ethical standards

EASJ INC does not accept donations that conflict with our mission, principles, or legal obligations. Support is evaluated on the basis of alignment with our values, integrity, and public-interest mandate.
